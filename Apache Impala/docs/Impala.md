### Books About Impala
Below are the first books on the market about Impala.
[](http://shop.oreilly.com/product/0636920033936.do)
#### [Getting Started with Impala](http://shop.oreilly.com/product/0636920033936.do)
by John Russell (O'Reilly Media, 2015)
Learn how to write, tune, and port SQL queries using Impala.
[](http://www.cloudera.com/content/cloudera/en/resources/library/aboutcloudera/cloudera-impala-ebook.html)
#### [Cloudera Impala](http://www.cloudera.com/content/cloudera/en/resources/library/aboutcloudera/cloudera-impala-ebook.html)
by John Russell (O'Reilly Media, 2013)
Compact primer for people from the RDBMS world. Also [in Japanese](http://www.oreilly.co.jp/books/9784873116723/).
[](http://www.packtpub.com/using-cloudera-impala/book)
#### [Learning Cloudera Impala](http://www.packtpub.com/using-cloudera-impala/book)
by Avkash Chauhan (Packt, 2014)
For Hadoop and Impala beginners.
