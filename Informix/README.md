# Informix

Informix is a relational and multi-model database management system with object-relational capabilities and support for JSON documents and time-series data. It supports transactional applications, analytics, and deployment within embedded applications, on edge devices, and on servers.

A secure embeddable database from IBM, positioned besides IBM Db2 as a relatively low-cost product optimized for OLTP and Internet of Things data

## 基本信息

- **数据库类型**: SQL、文档、空间、时序
- **所属机构**: IBM, HCL Technologies Effective May 1st, 2017, HCL took on development, technical support, and product management teams, and works jointly with IBM on product strategy, marketing, and sales.
- **许可证**: Proprietary
- **首次发布**: 1984
- **国家/地区**: US
